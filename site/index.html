03 / A few clear answers

Before you join.

Can I run a finished agent today?

Not yet. This repository is a foundation with contract templates and contribution infrastructure. Runnable agents, model adapters and packages are future contributions, not shipped capabilities.

Do I need a paid API key?

No key is needed for the website, documentation or launch regression tests. Future integrations must declare requirements and provide mocks for testing without real credentials.

Can students take part?

Yes. Start with the voluntary contribution pathway. There is no promise of paid work, academic credit or a job; tasks and reviewer availability are agreed first.

Who reviews and merges work?

Human maintainers review contributions under the governance rules. The automated operator never approves or merges its own pull requests.
