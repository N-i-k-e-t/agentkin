Family of AI Agents / foundation

A family built by contributors.

An opt-in place to learn, review and improve one useful piece at a time.

Where conversation lives today

Use the repository issues for bugs and concrete proposals, and pull requests for review. No official FOA Discord server, event calendar or university partnership is claimed today.

Student contribution pathway

Start with orientation, choose a realistic track and agree a small task with a willing reviewer. Continue through a PR, feedback and reflection. This is voluntary open-source participation; employment, payment, academic credits and certificates require separate confirmed terms.

Read the four-week pathway and private tracking rules ↗

Respect other communities

We respect the rules and consent preferences of other communities. We do not scrape member lists, send bulk unsolicited messages or imply partnerships that do not exist.

Public credit, private records

Public GitHub contributions provide evidence of work. Applications, email addresses, private feedback and participant tracking stay outside public Git history. Public attribution uses a consenting handle.
