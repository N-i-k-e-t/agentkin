## Research procedure

1. Pick one lead with a real task ready and a reviewer available.
2. Read its CONTRIBUTING, code of conduct and AI-assistance policy; comply with all of them.
3. Identify a recent relevant artifact and its actual author. Verify the exact public handle from that artifact. Avoid harvesting emails from commits or member lists.
4. Check if that person has invited feedback/collaboration or provided an appropriate public contact route. Otherwise do not DM; use a permitted public community area instead.
5. Record evidence URL/date, observation, fit score, channel-rule evidence and proposed task. Score 0–2 each for task fit, artifact recency, explicit contact permission and available reviewer. Do not treat score as permission.
6. Apply the one-message-ever and do-not-contact checks. Prepare G1 with a specific first line. Required fields must be filled before a send.

## University/club route

Start with an institution's official club page or GitHub organization; verify the coordinator's role through that page. Offer a 45-minute optional orientation and one 30–90 minute issue, scaled to skill level. Do not promise placements, academic credit, paid work or guaranteed acceptance.
